# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no frameworks (user decision). Deliverable folder: `marfil/`. Trilingual ES / EN / ZH (Simplified Chinese, Mandarin) via `i18n.js`; Chinese loads Noto Sans/Serif SC on demand.

Blog: articles edited by the client in Sveltia CMS at `/admin` (admins only: Cloudflare Access + fail-closed Pages Function + GitHub write access), stored as `content/blog/*.json` and rendered to static pages by `tools/build_pages.py` on each Cloudflare Pages build. Visitors can only read. Setup and daily use: `ADMIN-BLOG.md`.

## Users

B2B buyers of vegetable-tanned leather: footwear (incl. western boots), leather goods, belt and saddlery manufacturers and brands in Mexico, the US (Texas), South America, Europe and Asia. Decision makers: production heads, purchasing directors, designers, brand directors. They want consistent quality, sustainability certifications and reliable delivery.

## Product Purpose

Corporate site for Marfil Tannery (León, Guanajuato, since 1982) to make the company known and generate qualified leads through a quote form. Success = a buyer submits the form.

## Positioning

100% vegetable-tanned leather (pit-and-drum process), Leather Working Group certified, on-site water treatment, solar power, rainwater recovery. Family heritage in leather since c. 1950; tannery inaugurated in 1982 by President Miguel de la Madrid.

## Brand Commitments

Source: `marca/Manual de marca - Marfil.pdf` (Bistrea). Binding:
- Colours: #2F3E46 (Cool Gray), #354F52 (7545 C), #84A98C (5565 C), #CAD2C5 (5787 C). No other accent colours.
- Typefaces: Baskervville (primary), Montserrat (secondary).
- Logo: horizontal variation (bull + MARFIL + "Vegetable Tanned Leather", "Tanned" in sage); elements may be used independently; optional LWG seal. Never rotate, distort, change its fonts or add elements inside the safety margin. Minimum width 4 cm. Vectors in `marca/vectores/`.
- Icons: Precisión, Sustentabilidad, ¿Cambiamos el mundo? (duotone: sage line + pale offset).
- Textures: leather first, wood as complement, neutral plaster/smooth grounds.
- Slogan "Vegetable Tanned Leather"; sign-off "Tanning the future".
- Tone: honest, educational; corporate when selling, warm and human when communicating. Archetypes: Creator + Caregiver.
- Values: Sostenibilidad, Calidad, Innovación, Transparencia.
